# Refunds

## Step 1: Request a refund

The customer opens the order and clicks "Request refund". The form asks for a reason.

## Step 2: Check the order

Support opens the ticket, checks the order age (30 days), the item state and the reason.

## Step 3: Approve or reject

Support approves a request inside the policy. Anything else goes to "Reject" with a reason to the customer.

## Step 4: Issue the refund

Finance issues the refund to the original payment method within 3 working days.

## Step 5: Notify the customer

Support closes the ticket. The customer gets the confirmation email with the amount and the date.

## Rejected

The customer gets the rejection reason and can reply within 7 days.
