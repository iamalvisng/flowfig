# Returns

Roles: the customer, a support agent, the warehouse, finance.

## Step 1: Open a return

The customer opens a return from the order page within 30 days of delivery and picks a reason.

## Step 2: Review the request

A support agent checks the delivery date and the reason. A request after 30 days is rejected (see "Rejected").

## Step 3: Send the label

Support emails a prepaid shipping label to the customer.

## Step 4: Ship the item

The customer ships the item with the label within 14 days.

## Step 5: Inspect the item

The warehouse inspects the item within 2 working days of arrival. A damaged item goes to "Rejected".

## Step 6: Refund

Finance refunds the original payment method within 3 working days of a passed inspection.

## Step 7: Close

Support closes the ticket and the customer gets a confirmation email.

## Rejected

Support emails the reason. The customer can reply within 7 days. The warehouse ships a damaged item back.
